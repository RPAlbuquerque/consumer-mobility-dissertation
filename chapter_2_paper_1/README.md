# Chapter 2 / Paper 1: Systematic Literature Review

## Consumer Mobility and Localized Market Performance: A Systematic Review with an Integrative Synthesis of Market Exposure and Conversion

This directory contains the public review documentation for Chapter 2 of the dissertation. The study examines how marketing and adjacent literatures have connected consumer movement with market-relevant encounters, localized opportunities, and market outcomes across places.

## Research question

> How have marketing and adjacent literatures conceptualized and operationalized consumer mobility as market exposure, and how is this exposure translated into localized market opportunity and performance across places?

## Role in the dissertation

Paper 1 provides the conceptual foundation for the dissertation by separating four stages that are often treated as equivalent in mobility analytics: consumer movement, market exposure, localized market opportunity, and localized market performance. The integrative synthesis organizes these stages through the exposure-conversion framework and identifies local conversion conditions and latent market infrastructure as concepts for explaining why comparable exposure may correspond to different outcomes across places.

## Review design

The study combines a systematic literature review with an integrative synthesis. Scopus and Web of Science were searched on May 15, 2026, using three connected blocks covering mobility and location traces, consumer and commercial contexts, and exposure, opportunity, conversion, or performance outcomes.

| Review stage | Records |
|---|---:|
| Records identified in Scopus before additional filters | 1,261 |
| Records identified in Web of Science before additional filters | 574 |
| Records exported from Scopus after database filters | 521 |
| Records exported from Web of Science after database filters | 290 |
| Total exported records | 811 |
| Cross-database duplicates removed | 212 |
| Unique records screened | 599 |
| Records excluded after screening | 513 |
| Studies retained for the integrative synthesis | 86 |

The retained studies were organized according to their mobility construct, data regime, exposure construct, conversion outcome, and local conversion condition. This coding allowed evidence from retailing, location-based marketing, services, local consumption, commercial districts, urban planning, and spatial analytics to be compared within a common theoretical structure.
