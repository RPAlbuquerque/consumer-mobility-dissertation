# Chapter 2 / Paper 1: Systematic-Integrative Review

## When Does Consumer Mobility Relate to Local Sales? A Systematic-Integrative Review and Conditional Mobility–Sales Framework

This directory contains the public review documentation for Chapter 2 of the dissertation. The study examines under what local conditions observed consumer mobility relates to local sales across places.

## Research question

> Under what local conditions does observed consumer mobility relate to local sales?

## Role in the dissertation

Paper 1 provides a systematic-integrative synthesis of evidence on consumer mobility and local sales. It distinguishes consumer mobility, market exposure, localized market opportunity, local conversion conditions, and local sales without treating them as interchangeable measures or stages of one universal causal process.

Consumer mobility refers to observed movement, visits, proximity, and flows across or within places. Market exposure refers to the possibility that such movement brings consumers into contact with a focal offering. Localized market opportunity refers to the marketing assessment of whether that offering can satisfy buyer need profitably in a defined local market. Local sales are the focal observed outcome: realized purchases and spending associated with a focal offering in a defined local market and period. Local conversion conditions qualify whether potential contact becomes commercially viable and, in some cases, becomes local sales.

The review makes a summarizing contribution by organizing what heterogeneous studies establish about these analytical roles. It makes an integrating contribution by showing why consumer mobility is a conditional signal of local sales: potential contact may not result in purchase, comparable mobility may be associated with different local sales, and apparent sales gains may reflect reallocation across firms or channels rather than expanded local demand.

## Review design

The study combines a systematic literature review with an integrative synthesis. Scopus and Web of Science were searched on May 15, 2026, using three connected blocks covering mobility and location traces, consumer and commercial contexts, and terms broad enough to retrieve evidence on exposure, opportunity, conversion, sales, performance, spending, and place-based heterogeneity.

| Review stage | Records |
|---|---:|
| Records identified in Scopus before additional filters | 1,261 |
| Records identified in Web of Science before additional filters | 574 |
| Records exported from Scopus after database filters | 521 |
| Records exported from Web of Science after database filters | 290 |
| Total exported records | 811 |
| Cross-database duplicates removed | 212 |
| Unique records screened | 599 |
| Records excluded after screening | 513 |
| Studies retained for the integrative synthesis | 86 |

The retained studies were organized according to the form of evidence observed, its operationalization and data source, spatial unit, focal outcome, and local conversion conditions. This coding distinguished what each study observed from what it could establish about local sales and allowed heterogeneous evidence to be compared within the conditional mobility–sales framework.
