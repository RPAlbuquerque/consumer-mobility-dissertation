# Coding Framework for the Integrative Synthesis

## Purpose

The coding framework organized heterogeneous studies around the review question without treating consumer mobility, market exposure, demand, conversion, and performance as interchangeable constructs.

## Core analytical sequence

```text
consumer mobility -> market exposure -> local conversion conditions -> localized market opportunity and performance
```

## Primary coding dimensions

| Dimension | Coding question | Illustrative content |
|---|---|---|
| Mobility construct | What form of movement, visitation, location, or access does the study examine? | Foot traffic, store visits, customer flows, GPS traces, point-of-interest visits, proximity, catchments, or trade areas. |
| Data regime | How is mobility or place-related behavior observed? | Visitation and traffic measures, location traces and sensors, transactions and spending flows, or digital-place traces. |
| Exposure construct | How does movement create a market-relevant encounter possibility? | Access to stores, services, promotions, offerings, commercial districts, or place-based information. |
| Conversion outcome | What localized opportunity or outcome is connected with exposure? | Patronage, purchase, spending, demand, sales, revenue, profitability, business survival, or commercial vitality. |
| Local conversion condition | What condition is associated with whether exposure becomes opportunity or performance? | Digital information, accessibility, infrastructure, competition, promotion, service, spatial context, or commercial organization. |

## Supporting fields

The synthesis also recorded bibliographic information, disciplinary field, spatial scale, data type, method, theory, proposed mechanism, primary synthesis domain, and contribution to the exposure-conversion framework.

## Coding discipline

- Mobility was not coded as demand unless the study directly measured demand.
- Visitation was not coded as performance unless the study connected it with a realized market outcome.
- Digital or location-based activity was coded according to what the measure observed rather than its technological label.
- Causal language was retained only when supported by the design of the original study.
- Adjacent studies were retained only when their conceptual, empirical, or methodological contribution transferred to the review question.

## Synthesis domains

1. Retail traffic, visits, and store conversion.
2. Local consumption and spending flows.
3. Commercial districts, market areas, and spatial infrastructure.
4. Location-based marketing and digital conversion.
5. Services, hospitality, and non-retail local markets.
6. General mobility-market theory and methods.
