# Exclusion Criteria

Records were excluded when they failed the bibliographic or substantive requirements of the review.

## Bibliographic exclusions

- Not an English-language journal article within the database-export scope.
- Duplicate or superseded record.
- Insufficient bibliographic information for screening.

## Substantive exclusions

- No relevant mobility, visitation, place, location, or access construct.
- No consumer, customer, marketing, service, commercial, retail, or local-market construct.
- No sufficient connection with market exposure, localized opportunity, localized performance, or local conversion conditions.
- Mobility or access mentioned only as background rather than used conceptually, empirically, or analytically.
- Sales, demand, spending, or performance discussed without a mobility, visitation, access, or place-based exposure component.
- Transportation, tourism, health, sustainability, logistics, infrastructure, smart-city, or technical-computing research with no transferable contribution to the review question.
- Pure algorithmic or methodological work without a substantive interpretation for consumers, firms, services, commercial places, or localized markets.

## Final exclusion codes

| Code | Exclusion reason |
|---|---|
| `R1` | No relevant mobility or place construct. |
| `R2` | No market, consumer, commercial, service, or local-market construct. |
| `R3` | No sufficient exposure, opportunity, performance, or local-conversion connection. |
| `R4` | Out-of-scope adjacent issue or insufficient transferability to the review question. |
