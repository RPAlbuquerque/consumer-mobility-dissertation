# Inclusion Criteria

An article was retained when it satisfied all required criteria and contributed substantively to the dissertation's review question.

## Required criteria

1. The record was an English-language journal article within the database-export scope.
2. The article examined a mobility, visitation, location, place, or access construct, including consumer mobility, foot traffic, visits, customer flows, location traces, point-of-interest activity, spatial access, proximity, trade areas, or catchments.
3. The article addressed a consumer, customer, marketing, service, commercial, retail, or local-market phenomenon.
4. The article helped explain or measure at least one part of the relationship among consumer mobility, market exposure, localized market opportunity, local conversion conditions, and localized market performance.

## Contribution criteria

Eligible articles contributed to one or more of the following:

- conceptualization of mobility as exposure to firms, offerings, services, commercial areas, or consumption opportunities;
- operationalization of mobility-based exposure through visitation, foot traffic, mobile-location records, GPS, sensors, customer flows, or point-of-interest activity;
- connection of mobility or exposure with patronage, spending, demand, sales, conversion, business survival, or another localized market outcome;
- explanation of spatial, digital, infrastructural, operational, competitive, or contextual conditions associated with differences between exposure and outcomes; or
- transferable theory or measurement logic concerning store choice, trade areas, accessibility, commercial districts, place-based consumption, or spatially heterogeneous markets.

Articles from adjacent fields were eligible when their evidence or theory could be transferred to the marketing problem examined in the review. Retail language or the use of mobility data alone did not guarantee inclusion.
